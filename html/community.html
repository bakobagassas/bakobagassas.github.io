Community

How I lead and show up for people

Leadership, to me, means showing up consistently for communities I care about, whether that is mentoring girls through Relvox, building spaces for underrepresented students in tech through NSBE and WiCyS, or supporting my peers through philanthropy work at Berea. These roles all come back to the same idea: opportunity multiplies when someone opens the door for you, and you turn around to open it for someone else.

Founder Relvox · Niamey, Niger Mar 2024 – Present

An inclusive organization for women and youth empowerment in Niger. Since founding it, I have directed a team of 10 young women delivering programs to over 100 participants through summer camps and community initiatives, including mentorship programs, safe spaces, career development resources, and networking opportunities for girls and local youth.
